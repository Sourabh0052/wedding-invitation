Put your original photos here (JPG / PNG / WebP, straight from the phone is fine).

Then run:   npm run photos

Optimised copies are written to public/images/photos/ and the script prints the
lines to paste into src/config/weddingConfig.js.

Suggested names (any names work):
  gaurav-sheetal.jpg   → portrait of Gaurav & Sheetal
  sourav-komal.jpg     → portrait of Sourav & Komal
  anything-else.jpg    → goes in the "Our Moments" gallery

This folder is not uploaded to GitHub/Vercel (originals are large); only the
optimised copies in public/images/photos/ are published.
